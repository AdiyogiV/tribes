📋 Strategy — ranked by data leverage

  1. Go Hindi-first. Detect language, answer in it. Biggest invisible churn cause.
  2. Make the FIRST answer perfect. 46% of convos are one question — that reply is the whole funnel. In-language, chart-grounded, about timing, with a hook to Q2.
  3. Lean into voice. Where power users & richest questions live. Store + use transcripts (local whisper = $0).
  4. Specialize: Timing → Career → Marriage → Children. Depth over breadth.
  5. Interview the 6 power users. They cracked the loop — replicate it.
  6. Then wire analytics to track language, first→second-question conversion, chart-grounding rate.
