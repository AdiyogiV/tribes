Executive Insights

  • 🐄 HolyCow IS the product. 69 of 231 real users (29.9%) used the AI; AI conversations outnumber human DMs ~11:1.

  • 🚪 Activation leaks early. Only 22.5% finish FTUE; 57.1% have astro data. The funnel loses most people before the magic.
  • 🔮 What they want from us (the astrologers): Timing/prediction, Career & money, and Love/marriage dominate the readable questions.

So what? — Recommended next moves

  1. Fix the voice blind spot first. Transcribe & store voice queries. 37% of demand is invisible to us — this is the single highest-leverage data fix.
  2. Treat D1 retention as THE metric. 87% one-and-done means the loop never forms. Instrument & A/B the first-session experience before building new features (YAGNI on everything else).
  3. Mine the power users. 6 people drive 74.0% of usage — interview them, find the "aha", replicate it for the other 90%.
  4. Double down on Timing, Career, Love. That's what people actually ask. Make HolyCow exceptional at these three before breadth.
  5. Repair the activation funnel. <22.5% FTUE completion. Most never reach the astro setup that powers good answers.
  6. THEN wire lean analytics (the dead AnalyticsService) to track these specific funnels going forward — instrument what the data proved matters, not everything.
